How to read this

AdjO / AdjD: points scored and allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponent. Net is AdjO minus AdjD, the same idea as KenPom's AdjEM. AdjT is adjusted tempo, possessions per 40 minutes. Conf is the ESPN conference abbreviation. W-L is the record in the D1 games these ratings are built from, so W+L equals GP; non-D1 games are excluded.

ORtg (players) is a simplified approximation: points per 100 possessions used, with no assist credit, so it underrates playmakers. Read it with %Poss. RPR is our own single-number player rating, not KenPom's proprietary kPOY. Leaderboards only include players with at least 40% of their team's minutes, so a two-minute cameo cannot top the list.

Honest limits: 2026-27 is two to three games per team, so its order is mostly noise. The 2024-25 and 2025-26 seasons are full seasons. No KenPom data was used or scraped; his published rankings are only a benchmark to compare against.
