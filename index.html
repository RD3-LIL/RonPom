How to read this

AdjO / AdjD: points scored and allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponent. Net is AdjO minus AdjD. AdjT is adjusted tempo, possessions per 40 minutes.

ORtg (players) is a simplified approximation: points per 100 possessions used, with no assist credit, so it underrates playmakers. Read it with %Poss. RPR is RonPom's own single-number player rating, not KenPom's proprietary kPOY. Leaderboards only include players with at least 40% of their team's minutes, so a two-minute cameo cannot top the list.

Honest limits: 2026-27 is two games per team, so its order is mostly noise. The 2024-25 and 2025-26 seasons are full seasons. No KenPom data was used or scraped; his published rankings are only a benchmark to compare against.

Data: ESPN public scoreboard and box scores. Code and full CSVs: RD3-LIL/RonPom on GitHub. Built Nov 10, 2026. Not affiliated with KenPom.
