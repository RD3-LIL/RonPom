# Luck fix: stats-missing and unplayed games, 2026-11-20

## What was wrong

`extra_stats.compute_extra` counted every D1-vs-D1 game in the
log, including games the solver never sees:

- Games flagged `stats_missing` (ESPN returned a final score
  with an empty boxscore). The solver skips these (zero
  possessions), so Luck counted a game the ratings did not.
- Postponed 0-0 games (Nov 14-15, 2026). With both scores 0,
  `home_score > away_score` is False, so the away team was
  credited with a win it never played.

12 teams had Luck GP exactly 1 higher than solver GP. The
worst case was Air Force: Luck +0.428 on a 3-2 record that
included a phantom away win at Pacific (postponed, 0-0).

The fix (in `extra_stats.py`) skips any game with
`stats_missing` set, and any game with home_score 0 and
away_score 0. `compute_extra` now also returns `gp` and
`wins` per team, and `test_extra_stats.py` asserts Luck GP
equals solver GP on synthetic games and on the real 2026-27
data (10 tests, all passing).

Note: the two played Nov 10 games with empty stats
(Vanderbilt 105-81 Memphis, Iowa 88-93 Virginia Tech) were
re-fetched with full stats on Nov 20 (see the fetch fix),
so they now count in both the solver and Luck, with real
efficiencies. Their Luck barely moves, because the old code
already counted them with the correct W/L from the score;
the change for them is that AdjO/AdjD (and so expected W%)
now use real possessions.

Why the big movers moved: Louisiana and Pepperdine each
lost a phantom away win in a postponed 0-0 game (Louisiana
at UNC Wilmington, Pepperdine at Oregon), dropping to 0-3.
Oregon, Pacific, and UNC Wilmington each lost a phantom
0-0 loss at home, so their actual W% rose. Air Force lost
its phantom away win at Pacific, 3-2 becomes 2-2, Luck
+0.428 becomes +0.328, as expected.

SOS also shifts for the affected teams, since the phantom
games no longer contribute opponent Net (Air Force SOS
+0.5 becomes -0.5, Oregon SOS -7.3 becomes -7.4).

## Docstring correction

The old module docstring said an opponent with no rating
(non-D1) "counts as the mean Net of the rated teams". That
was false in code: such games are skipped outright for both
teams (`if home not in ratings or away not in ratings:
continue`). The docstring now says that. The mean-Net
fallback only applies to a team with no counted D1 games at
all, whose SOS defaults to the mean Net.
