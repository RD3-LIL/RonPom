## Read

Below the 0.7 rho target, as the rubric expects in week one, but the real prior moved it a lot: with the league-average prior rho was 0.342, overlap at 25 was 12 of 25, and mean rank difference was 21.9. With the real prior those are 0.542, 15 of 25, and 14.8. The remaining gap is mostly teams whose prior is weak because they returned almost nobody (Tennessee 0.09, Arizona 0.27, Texas Tech 0.21), plus one blowout artifact: LSU is our No. 1 on two big wins with a zero returning share, so nothing anchors them, and they are not in his top 25 at all. The gap should keep closing as games accumulate. If it has not closed by mid-season on a full game log, the solver or the prior is wrong.
