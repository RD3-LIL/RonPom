Distributions

Pick a stat and see how all 5123 players are spread across it. The bars are a histogram of the real players, and the dark line is a smooth curve that follows the shape of the data. The dashed grey line is the mean.

These distributions cover every D1 player with a value for that stat, not just the top-25 teams. Only players on top-25 teams, plus the top 150 by RPR, have a profile page, so a player without a profile shows as plain text in the bin lists. Per-40 stats use qualified players only (min 2 games, 40% of team minutes), so a one-minute cameo cannot top the chart.

Hover or tap a bar to see the players in that range.
